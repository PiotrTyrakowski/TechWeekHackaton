# Research-derived demo opportunities

These are product/pitch hypotheses for the separate workstream, not validated
business outcomes or claims to judging points. No frontend work is authorized by
this note.

- **Decision-history assistant:** show what changed, when, and the conversations
  supporting the current plan. Dates/provenance and update retrieval already
  exist; reliable final citations and conflict resolution need live validation.
- **Evidence budget compiler:** turn a large project history into a small,
  auditable evidence packet. The compact selector demonstrates a measurable
  retrieval improvement with no neural inference dependency. It does not yet
  establish better Reactor answers.
- **Temporal project handoff:** reconstruct commitments and milestones across
  conversations, then expose arithmetic with source anchors. Current temporal
  recall is a bottleneck; deterministic event extraction and supported interval
  calculations remain research, not a finished application.

The provided 4/4/4 judging split motivates these narratives, but the relationship
between benchmark performance and presentation points remains unspecified.
